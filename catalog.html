Our Catalog

Six woods, six moods

Every L'Artisan kit ships with all six flavours. Here's what each one brings to the glass — and the pours it was made for.

Oak

Pure oak · rich & smooth

Deep, warm and classic — the backbone of barrel aging. Rounds out the spirit with a slow, woody sweetness.

Best with · Old Fashioned · Neat bourbon · Rye

Cherry

Sweet & mild

Fruity and fragrant with a soft finish. Lifts stirred and fruit-forward drinks without overpowering them.

Best with · Manhattan · Dark rum · Amaro

Pecan

Nutty & rich

Toasty, full and a little sweet. A rich, rounded smoke that flatters darker, aged spirits.

Best with · Neat whiskey · Aged rum · Old Fashioned

Apple

Fruity & light

Mild, mellow and gently sweet. The easy option when you want aroma without weight.

Best with · Tequila · Gin · White rum

Peach

Sweet & smooth

Soft stone-fruit sweetness with a smooth, warm finish. Made for sippable, sweeter cocktails.

Best with · Bourbon sour · Whiskey · Peach cocktails

Pear

Mild & refreshing

Light, clean and delicate. A refreshing smoke that keeps bright drinks bright.

Best with · Vodka · Gin · Light cocktails
