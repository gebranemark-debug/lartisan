Good to know

Questions

How does the smoker kit actually work?
Load a pinch of wood chips onto the smoker top, light them with the torch, and rest it over your filled glass. Cap it, let the smoke infuse for 20–30 seconds, then lift and sip. The whole ritual takes under a minute.
Is the torch included? Does it come with butane?
The torch is included in every kit. For safety reasons butane gas can't be shipped, so you'll fill the refillable torch with any standard butane, available at most tobacco or hardware shops in Lebanon.
How do I pay? Is it safe?
Cash on delivery — you pay the courier when the box reaches your door. No card, no online payment, nothing upfront. You only pay once it's in your hands.
Where do you deliver, and how long does it take?
We deliver across all of Lebanon. Delivery time depends on your area, and we'll confirm the timing with you directly on WhatsApp when you place your order.
Can I order it as a gift?
Absolutely — the engraved oak case is made to be gifted. Let us know on WhatsApp and we can arrange delivery straight to the person you're gifting it to.
What's the Oak Club?
A monthly membership that delivers a new wood flavour to your door each month, with a pairing card for the spirit to match. No card, no lock-in — we message you before each delivery, you confirm, and pay on arrival. Cancel any time.
